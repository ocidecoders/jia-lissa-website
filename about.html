Who is Jia Lissa?

A Russian model and artist whose work moves between editorial fashion and quiet portraiture. Known for her signature red hair and a warmth that translates through every frame, Jia has built a body of work that blends elegance with an unmistakable European sensibility.

Her photography and modeling work has appeared across major international publications and continues to grow — always guided by the same principle: that beauty lives in the quiet, the honest, and the thoughtful.
