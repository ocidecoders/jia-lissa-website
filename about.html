Who is Jia Lissa?

A Russian model and actress whose work spans European editorial and cinematography, known for her signature red hair and a career that has earned multiple international awards.

Born in Izhevsk in 1996 and based in Moscow since 2013, Jia has built a body of work that blends elegance with an unmistakable European sensibility - always guided by the same principle: that beauty lives in the quiet, the honest, and the thoughtful.
